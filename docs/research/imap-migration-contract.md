# Staged IMAP migration contract

Investigated 2026-10-17 using RFCs and FRY's current collector/storage source. Scope: a bounded baseline migration prototype and its verification seams, not a production cutover or a demonstrated 30-minute capacity result. [ADR 0002](../adr/0002-direct-and-imap-ingress.md) and [ADR 0014](../adr/0014-prove-conventional-email-first.md) require background history import, preserved folders/dates/flags, at most 30 minutes of final service interruption, and no source deletion. New destination UIDs and coordinated client checkpoint reset are acceptable; client resynchronization can continue after FRY resumes service.

## Protocol evidence and limits

A source message identity includes account, mailbox name, UIDVALIDITY, and UID. UIDVALIDITY changes invalidate saved UID associations. UIDNEXT bounds subsequent arrivals; it does not promise that every lower UID exists. Under a stable identity, raw message content and INTERNALDATE are immutable, but flags can change. A missing UID FETCH can succeed with no message. UID SEARCH still interprets bare ranges as sequence numbers: use its UID criterion. A range ending in `*` can unexpectedly include the last message when its other endpoint exceeds the maximum. [RFC 3501 §2.3.1.1 and §6.4.8](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc3501.html#section-2.3.1.1).

Fetch FLAGS, INTERNALDATE, RFC822.SIZE, UID, and BODY.PEEK[]; do not derive INTERNALDATE from the Date header. PEEK avoids setting Seen. Preserve Deleted as a flag for messages still present; it does not mean expunged. Recent is session/server state, not a migration flag to restore. A literal's advertised byte length is not the whole command: verify framing and successful tagged completion as well as UID attribution. Servers can send unsolicited changes between commands. [RFC 9051 §2.3.2–2.3.3, §4.3 and §6.4.5](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9051.html#section-6.4.5).

Baseline IMAP does not reliably identify a renamed folder across independent listings. Equal UIDVALIDITY values across different names do not establish the same folder. OBJECTID optionally adds stable MAILBOXID and message EMAILID, specifically to improve recognition across rename/copy. [RFC 8474 §3–4](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8474.html#section-4).

CONDSTORE offers modification sequences and CHANGEDSINCE to reduce flag rescans; NOMODSEQ requires fallback. QRESYNC adds efficient resynchronization including vanished UIDs, with UIDVALIDITY and known-UID state participating in the request. Neither extension freezes other clients, SMTP delivery, administrators, or all folders together. Their optimization does not establish a migration barrier. [RFC 7162 §3.1–3.2](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc7162.html#section-3).

**Engineering conclusion:** identical repeated LIST/UID inventories, unchanged UIDNEXT, NOOP, and successful FETCH completions are observations, not proof that another writer cannot act immediately afterward. A final migration needs a source-side admission/write barrier supplied by deployment orchestration. An operator's statement that this barrier exists must be recorded separately from what FRY observed.

## Current implementation seams

The ongoing [collector](../../internal/imapcollect/collector.go) deliberately skips accepted source identities and does not mirror subsequent flags/moves/deletions. It currently searches the whole folder, then limits expanded results to one million; MaxMessages limits copies, not search response allocation. Its metadata/body streaming and `literalSource` completion checks are useful foundations, but a successful collection pass is not a migration reconciliation pass.

[SourceAttempt/AcceptedSources](../../internal/receiptstore/source_linux.go) provide durable repeat detection, with original immutable acceptance metadata. [Source](../../internal/receiptstore/types.go) keys on connection/folder/UIDVALIDITY/UID; connection binding prevents account reuse under one identity. `Envelope.MailboxAppend` publishes the explicit destination membership atomically with acceptance, and [Appended](../../internal/receiptstore/mailbox_linux.go) returns the committed placement even after later expunge. MailboxAppend currently disallows Source; do not simply combine both modes. [Acceptance transaction](../../internal/receiptstore/postgres.go).

The local pinned `go-imap/v2 v2.0.0-beta.8` client supports `SelectOptions{ReadOnly:true, CondStore:true}`, `SelectData.UIDValidity/UIDNext/HighestModSeq`, `SearchCriteria.UID`, and `FetchOptions.ChangedSince/ModSeq`. Its SELECT encoder has no QRESYNC parameter and its client has no VANISHED handling found in this inspection. Do not infer QRESYNC support from capability constants. [Select API](../../third_party/go-imap/select.go), [encoder](../../third_party/go-imap/imapclient/select.go), [search](../../third_party/go-imap/search.go), [fetch](../../third_party/go-imap/fetch.go). ## Proposed bounded baseline

1. **Plan and isolate.** Persist a job with source-account fingerprint, organization, approved folder mapping, destination identities, limits, and state version. Discover selectable folders and hierarchy separately; record subscriptions if included in the migration scope. Require complete account visibility or explicitly scope the migration to an allowlist. Use migration-owned destinations inaccessible to clients and normal ingress during copying. Reject ambiguous destination/name collisions rather than merging into an active mailbox.
2. **Inventory by epoch and finite windows.** EXAMINE each folder; require valid UIDVALIDITY and UIDNEXT. Capture `upper = UIDNEXT - 1`, skipping an empty range when UIDNEXT is 1. Search finite windows with `SearchCriteria{UID: []imap.UIDSet{...}}`; never use `*` or issue a reversed empty range. Bound window width, folder count, concurrent fetches, message bytes, command time, and job work per run. Persist completed windows and individual pending UIDs. Advance progress only after every observed UID has a durable copy or an explicit rechecked disappearance. Sparse UID spaces may require many windows: measure this before scheduling cutover.
3. **Copy with a durable ledger.** Key entries by job, source-folder epoch, and UID. Persist a stable attempt ID and immutable acceptance envelope before calling Accept; retry that attempt after an ambiguous result. A separate migration ledger can use MailboxAppend without Source, then recover placement with Status/Appended. Never infer success solely from EOF or a historical marker whose destination membership disappeared. Verify expected UID even when the UID item follows the literal. Preserve raw bytes, date, and persistent flags/keywords. Identical bytes or Message-ID values do not collapse distinct source memberships.
4. **Reconcile in staging.** Completed inventory generations mark exact presence. Update all surviving flags, including removal of formerly set flags. Remove only migration-owned destination memberships proven absent in a completed source epoch; never infer deletion from a failed/partial LIST, SEARCH, or FETCH. A changed UIDVALIDITY invalidates that folder's previous scan and mapping: stage a new epoch and supersede old memberships after completion. A baseline source rename can be represented as old-name disappearance plus new-name import; do not guess identity from matching UIDVALIDITY. This may recopy large history and makes late namespace changes a cutover readiness concern. Source messages are never deleted.
5. **Prepare final reconciliation before pausing.** Rehearse inventory/metadata throughput, remaining copy bytes, storage headroom, and destination verification at representative scale. Reduce residual work through additional background passes. A bounded implementation can stop/resume safely without proving it can finish a huge mailbox in 30 minutes. Baseline metadata scanning is O(number of messages), even if no bytes remain to copy.
6. **Final barrier and handoff.** Record an authorized freeze attestation covering scope, time, identity, clients, old delivery paths, administrative jobs, and how queued arrivals will be handed off. Validate observable source inventory again after the attested barrier; complete residual copies and exact flag/presence reconciliation. Re-list and reselect as a consistency check; any contradiction invalidates readiness. Verify destination counts, mappings, sizes/dates/flags, and stored content digests. Persist a completion manifest and transition authority once before enabling FRY clients/ingress. Stop migration writes permanently after handoff. Reset client checkpoints through the planned operational procedure.

The 30-minute clock starts with the first affected service pause and includes final synchronization and switching. Reserve explicit time for cancellation and restoration of the old service if readiness fails; an aborted cutover is not a successful FRY rollout. Do not automatically route traffic back after FRY has begun accepting new state without reconciling that state. DNS changes alone do not drain old SMTP paths; delayed arrivals need an agreed queue/forwarding arrangement. These are operational contract requirements, not facilities provided by the IMAP protocol.

Finite-window requests bound honest-server result cardinality; the client still parses responses before FRY's post-validation. Strict protection from an adversarial overlong SEARCH/LIST response needs parser/transport limits as well as request limits. Keep this distinct from message streaming bounds. Paginated SQL ledger reads are also necessary: current `MailboxSelection.View()` loads an entire destination folder and is not a bounded migration inventory primitive. [Current selection API](../../internal/receiptstore/selection_linux.go).

## Module boundary and tests

Prefer `imapmigrate` as a job coordinator over a reusable authenticated source reader and a receiptstore migration-ledger API. The source reader returns bounded inventory pages and verified literal streams; it cannot attest a freeze. The ledger owns claims, attempt recovery, destination mapping, reconciliation generations, and handoff fencing. Store policy must forbid cleanup of memberships not owned by this job. Use a durable state/version guard to prevent two coordinators or a delayed pre-handoff worker from modifying a handed-off mailbox.

Meaningful local tests should exercise a real TCP source and destination/store, plus a scripted faulty source:

- Background copy, then source flag additions/removals, append, expunge, folder create/rename/delete, and final reconciliation: compare the entire scoped mailbox state; retain source history unchanged by the migrator.
- UID holes, empty folders, high/sparse UIDs, reversed-range traps, UIDVALIDITY reset with reused UIDs, and two folders sharing UIDVALIDITY; no incorrect association or gap skipping.
- Distinct messages with identical bytes and Message-ID; retain distinct memberships. Deleted-but-unexpunged messages remain present with their flag.
- UID after body, missing UID, wrong size, short literal, valid bytes followed by tagged NO or connection loss: no acceptance of unverified transfer. Retry after accepted-but-unrecorded placement creates no duplicate.
- Partial inventory or metadata failure never triggers deletion. Crash between attempt allocation, acceptance, placement recording, reconciliation, and handoff remains resumable and idempotent.
- Source changes after two identical scans: no claimed barrier from scans alone. Missing/stale/wrong-scope attestation cannot authorize handoff; observed changes contradicting an attestation block it.
- Timeout/abort and stale worker after handoff cannot mutate active destination state. Inject a fake clock for deadline state transitions, but separately benchmark real final-pass duration; a fake-clock test does not validate the 30-minute target.
